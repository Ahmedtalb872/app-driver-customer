تطبيق الهدهد (Al Hodhod) — com.alhudhud.customerapp

يمكنك حذف حسابك في تطبيق الهدهد وكل بياناتك الشخصية في أي وقت، بإحدى الطريقتين أدناه.

الطريقة الأولى: من داخل التطبيق (فورية)

  1. افتح تطبيق الهدهد وسجّل الدخول.
  2. اذهب إلى حسابي ← إعدادات التطبيق.
  3. اختر «حذف الحساب نهائياً» ضمن قسم الأمان والحساب.
  4. أكّد الحذف. يُحذف حسابك فوراً ولا يمكن الرجوع عن ذلك.

English

You can delete your Al Hodhod account (com.alhudhud.customerapp) and all personal data at any time:

  1. In the app: Profile → App Settings → "حذف الحساب نهائياً" (Delete account permanently) → confirm. Deletion is immediate.
  2. By email: write to ahmedtalb872@gmail.com with the phone number registered on the account. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Deleted permanently: your account and login, name, phone, email, profile photo, wallet and its transaction history, saved places, subscriptions, and your notification device token.

Retained but fully de-identified: completed trip records. They are also the assigned driver's own work and earnings history, so they are kept with the link to you removed - after deletion they contain no information identifying you.

Deletion is blocked while a trip is in progress or while a "Selefli" (pay-later) balance is still owed. Any remaining wallet balance is forfeited.
